    Little tips for big adventures

    ☀️

    When to go

    The dry season (roughly December to April) brings calm, sunny days. The wetter months (roughly May to November) are better for mantas at Hanifaru and for surf.

    🛩️

    Getting around

    Speedboats, seaplanes, domestic flights and slow dhoni ferries link the islands. On the islands themselves, buggies and bikes beat cars.

    🪸

    Be kind to the reef

    Use reef-safe sunscreen, never touch corals, turtles or sharks, and keep your distance from whale sharks.

    🕌

    Local islands vs hotels

    Hotels are one island each and are the place for alcohol and swimwear. On local islands dress modestly, use the bikini beach, and expect cheaper stays.
